Frequently Asked
Questions

What is Gelato?

Gelato is an alcoholic flavoured drink created for adults who enjoy bold flavour and refreshing serves.

What is the alcohol content?

Gelato contains 5% alcohol by volume (5% ABV).

What is the pack volume?

The product information supplied for this website indicates a net volume of 1 litre (1L).

How should I enjoy Gelato?

Serve chilled. If mixing, use clean glassware and fresh ingredients. Always drink responsibly.

Where can I find the full product information?

Please refer to the product label for the complete ingredients, storage instructions and regulatory information.
