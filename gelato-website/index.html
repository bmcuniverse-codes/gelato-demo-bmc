A little fruit knowledge

Goodness that starts with fruit.

Different fruits provide different nutrients. Here is a simple guide to vitamins and other nutrients commonly found in fruit. The exact amount in any Gelato drink depends on its fruit blend, recipe and processing.

C

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function, helps the body make collagen for skin and connective tissue, and improves absorption of iron from plant foods.

A

Vitamin A

Supports normal vision, immune function and the maintenance of healthy skin. Some fruits provide beta-carotene, which the body can convert to vitamin A.

B6

Vitamin B6

Helps the body use and store energy from food and supports normal brain and nervous-system function.

B9

Folate

Helps the body make healthy red blood cells and supports normal cell division and growth.

E

Vitamin E

Acts as an antioxidant, helping protect cells from oxidative damage. It is present in some fruits, including avocado.

K

Vitamin K

Contributes to normal blood clotting and helps maintain healthy bones. Some fruits, such as kiwifruit, provide vitamin K.

K⁺

Potassium

A mineral that supports normal muscle function, nerve signalling and the maintenance of normal blood pressure.

✦

Fibre & plant compounds

Whole fruits can provide dietary fibre for digestive health and plant compounds such as polyphenols that contribute to fruit colour and antioxidant activity.

Nutrition note: these are general facts about nutrients found in various fruits, not a verified nutrient analysis of Gelato. Do not describe a specific Gelato flavour as a source of a vitamin or as “high in” a nutrient until its final recipe and nutrition label have been confirmed. Fruit drinks may contain less fibre than whole fruit.

Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful details about enjoying Gelato.

What is Gelato?

Gelato is a sweet beverage produced from natural fruit sources. It is made to bring fruity refreshment to everyday moments.

How is Gelato best served?

Gelato is best served chilled. Refrigerate according to the storage instructions on the product packaging and enjoy it cool.

Does Gelato contain vitamins?

Fruits naturally contain different vitamins and minerals. The specific nutrients and quantities in a Gelato drink depend on the fruits used and the final recipe. Please refer to the product label for verified nutritional information.
